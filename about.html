History

We are a local kasi premium car wash and auto detailing. Majoring in car cleaning services like valet and removing of scratches through paint correction method. Also have services such as carpet/rug washing and upholstery. King Car Wash was founded in July 2025 as a one-person operation with no equipment of its own- just a strong ethic and a commitment to quality. In under a year, the business has grown from a single founder handling every wash by hand to a company renting a large operating space in its local town and employing four young men. This rapid growth reflects both rising demand for premium detailing in the community and the founder's dedication to building something that creates opportunity for others.

Our Mission

To provide clean, hygienic and disinfected vehicles to the community because people spend a significant portion of their lives driving, and their vehicles should never be a health hazard- while simultaneously tackling youth unemployment by creating jobs that give young people in the community a purposeful alternative to standing on street corners.

Vision

To create employment that feeds and provides a good life for the people of our community, and to inspire and encourage entrepreneurship among local youth through King Car Wash's own example of success.

Team members

We are a team of young men who are passionate about cars and car cleaning. We are committed to providing the best car cleaning services to our customers while also creating employment opportunities for young people in our community.

Target Audience

An elite, quality- conscious clientele who value exceptional, professional grade service and expect convenience- vehicle owners who want top tier detailing delivered at their doorstep, without needing to travel to receive it..
