Our History

King Car Wash was founded in July 2025 as a one-person operation with no equipment of its own- just a strong ethic and a commitment to quality. In under a year, the business has grown from a single founder handling every wash by hand to a company renting a large operating space in its local town and employing four young men. This rapid growth reflects both rising demand for premium detailing in the community and the founder's dedication to building something that creates opportunity for others.

Our Mission

To provide clean, hygienic and disinfected vehicles to the community because people spend a significant portion of their lives driving, and their vehicles should never be a health hazard- while simultaneously tackling youth unemployment by creating jobs that give young people in the community a purposeful alternative to standing on street corners.

Our Vision

To create employment that feeds and provides a good life for the people of our community, and to inspire and encourage entrepreneurship among local youth through King Car Wash's own example of success.

Our Team

Our team is composed of dedicated professionals who share our passion for excellence and customer satisfaction. We believe in fostering a collaborative environment where every team member can contribute to our collective success.
